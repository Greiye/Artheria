**World:** Artheria  
**Primary Setting:** Greyhaven, Veilshade Manor, Aralon, Ranlean, Amethyst Peak  
**Levels:** 5–12  
**Themes:** Identity, memory, redemption, consequences, moral ambiguity

---

## PHASE I: The Forgotten Village (Levels 5–7)

### **Act 1: Arrival in Greyhaven**

- The party meets **Alveus Zeheimer**, an eccentric scholar plagued by gaps in his memory.
    
- He invites them to Greyhaven, a misty town with eerie tranquility.
    
- The party begins noticing signs of something wrong: missing memories, hollow smiles, and whispered events no one recalls.
    
- Lake Mnemos reflects people as they _used_ to be—not as they are.
    

**Key Events:**

- Uncover fragmented history of the town via journals, distorted murals, and altered town records.
    
- Experience an **Echo Day** where citizens unknowingly relive past tragedies.
    
- Discover sealed doorways and ritual sites hinting at deeper secrets.
    

**Central Location:** Chapel, Town Hall, Stillmind Asylum, **Fountain covering the entrance to Veilshade Hall**.

---

## PHASE II: The Crypt of Echoes (Levels 7–9)

### **Act 2: Descent into Veilshade Hall**

- The party uncovers **Veilshade Hall/Manor**, a crumbling estate turned experimental lab.
    
- Beneath it lies the **Crypt of Echoes**, a facility where Dr. Z conducted his infamous memory manipulation experiments.
    
- Townspeople’s memory loss was not a curse—it was a treatment.
    

**Revelations:**

- Alveus was once known as **Dr. Z**, a brilliant but morally compromised Ranlean scholar.
    
- He created what locals call **Stillmind Syndrome**—an experimental "peace-inducing treatment" for trauma.
    
- Alveus voluntarily infected himself to forget the horrors of what he had done.
    

**Key Mechanics:**

- Memory Crystal Puzzles.
    
- Encounters with distorted echoes of former patients.
    
- Hallucination chambers where players experience _false versions_ of their pasts.
    

---

## PHASE III: Across the Tria Prima (Levels 9–11)

### **Act 3: Recovery or Ruin**

- With knowledge of the disease’s origin, the party travels through the Tria Prima cities:
    
    - **Aralon**: Find peaceful resistance to memory manipulation. NPCs may oppose restoring trauma.
        
    - **Ranlean**: Uncover forbidden research; possibly meet one of Dr. Z’s surviving colleagues.
        
    - **Amethyst Peak**: Learn how the Stillmind Syndrome was used to subdue warriors or suppress rebellion.
        

**Objectives:**

- Recover pieces of Dr. Z’s fractured journal.
    
- Determine whether curing the disease will bring healing—or collapse entire communities.
    
- Encounter infected NPCs whose personalities shift based on missing memories.
    

---

## PHASE IV: The Mind Forgotten (Levels 11–12)

### **Act 4: Final Confrontation**

- Alveus regains full memory. The guilt overwhelms him.
    
- He disappears into Veilshade Hall, which is now warped by his unraveling mind.
    

**Final Dungeon: Inside the Mind**

- Each room is a memory fragment, blending real and surreal environments.
    
- The party faces memory-based illusions, combat echoes of the past, and moral challenges.
    

**Boss Encounter: Dr. Z (Alveus Zeheimer Reawakened)**

- Psychic abilities, anti-healing zones, guilt projections.
    
- Health phases tied to specific memories: players can weaken him by resolving or confronting those memories.
    

**Endgame Choices:**

- **Restore Alveus**: He becomes a broken, but powerful force for good—or risks relapse.
    
- **Destroy Alveus**: End the cycle, but erase all remaining evidence.
    
- **Merge with Alveus**: One PC absorbs his knowledge, becoming the keeper of forbidden memory.
    

---

## Rewards & Legacy

- **Mnemonic Lens** (Magic Item): Relive a memory once per day.
    
- **Veilshade Sigil**: Grants access to lost lore and restricted Ranlean Tech.
    
- Unlocked Background: **Memory-Walker** – Advantage on Insight vs deception, plus one forgotten language.
    
- Global Consequences: If Alveus lives, the memory-affecting condition begins to spread across Artheria.

## Appendices

### Memory Echoes – Themed Creatures

- **Echo of Grief** (CR 4): Aura of psychic damage; appears as someone lost.
    
- **Echo of Regret** (CR 6): Uses Suggestion and Mirror Image; heals from hesitation.
    
- **Fractured Self** (CR 8): A corrupted mirror of a party member.
    
- **Mind Leech Prototype** (CR 10): On hit, forces target to forget their last turn.
    

### Memory Puzzle Mechanics

- **Memory Alignment**: Chronological or emotional ordering to unlock doors.
    
- **Hall of Truths**: Spot illusions in projected scenes.
    
- **Mnemonic Lock**: Emotion-based passcode puzzles.
    

### Stillmind Syndrome Stages

- **Stage I**: -1 to Insight/Investigation.
    
- **Stage II**: Time distortion; magic misfire.
    
- **Stage III**: Temporary personality shift.
    
- **Stage IV**: Loss of ability/spell/allied memory.
    

### Crypt of Echoes Map Sketch

- **Fountain Entrance**, **Observation Hall**, **Experimentation Ward**, **The Archive**, **Final Room: The Chair**
    

### Echo Day Encounter Table (1d6)

1. A wedding for a couple no one remembers.
    
2. A riot against an unnamed mayor.
    
3. Children reenacting a massacre with dolls.
    
4. A townwide prayer for a god no one worships.
    
5. A funeral with no body.
    
6. Townsfolk chanting Dr. Z’s old motto.
    

### Magical Items & Inventions

- **Stabilized Mercury Flask**: Grants advantage on INT saves for 1 hour.
    
- **Saltstone of Clarity**: Once/day cure memory confusion.
    
- **Sulfuric Incense of Truth**: Compels honest memories in a 10ft radius.
    
- **Liquid Recall (Potion)**: Restores one forgotten spell or feature.
    

### Handouts

- **Redacted Journal Pages** from Dr. Z.
    
- **Personal Memory Fragments** unique to each PC.
    
- **Town Records** with mismatched facts and scratched-out names.
    

### Optional Epilogues

- **Alveus Redeemed**: Becomes a sage and opens the Hall of Recollection.
    
- **Alveus Destroyed**: Memories flood back into the world, for better or worse.
    
- **Alveus Merged**: One PC inherits Dr. Z’s legacy, and possibly his madness.

### 🧭 Location

Nestled near the border between **Aralon** and **Ranlean**, Greyhaven is a secluded town shrouded in constant mist and early morning silence. It overlooks a glimmering silver lake—**Lake Mnemos**—whose waters are believed to reflect not just one's face, but fragments of one's past.

---

### 🏗️ Founding: The Quiet Promise

**Greyhaven** was founded nearly two centuries ago by a **splinter group of Ranlean scholars** and **Aralonian philosophers** seeking to combine technological advancement with spiritual peace. These pioneers believed that **memory** was the key to enlightenment—but also the root of pain.

So they built a village designed for **mental clarity** and **emotional healing**—a place where people could forget their trauma and start fresh. Their motto:

> _“To remember less is to suffer less.”_

Using a now-banned **Ranlean alchemical prototype**, they developed what would become the **precursor to Al Zeheimer’s Disease**—a "therapeutic memory dilution process" they called **Greywater Treatment**.

---

### 🔍 Central Mystery: The Stillmind Conspiracy

Over time, citizens stopped questioning their pasts. Generations grew up in peace—but **never left**, never asked about missing relatives, and never remembered birthdays, funerals, or anything traumatic. A strange **placidity** overtook Greyhaven.

Alveus Zeheimer was once a promising Ranlean scholar assigned to **study the after-effects** of the Greywater Treatment in Greyhaven. But in his obsession to **replicate and control** memory erasure, he secretly expanded the experiments. Unbeknownst to the town, **he succeeded**—creating Al Zeheimer’s Disease, which leaked into the water, infecting the whole region.

He infected **himself**, believing he could be reborn into innocence. And he was—now he wanders with the party as an eccentric, kind-hearted NPC.

But beneath the calm smiles of Greyhaven lies:

- Villagers who are **missing core memories**.
    
- A **sealed underground lab** under the town square.
    
- Whispered rumors of **“Echo Days”**—moments where people relive a forgotten memory in horrifying detail before losing it again.
    
- A journal in the chapel with **entire pages blacked out**—written by a priest who knew too much.
    
- A mural in the town hall that keeps **changing subtly each week**—portraits of people no one remembers.

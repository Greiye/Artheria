## **Lore Fuel (Unimplemented Storylines)**

### **The Grand Beholder**

A town is besieged by nightmarish monstrosities, their presence heralded by whispered legends of a lurking Grand Beholder. As terror grips the streets, the townsfolk speak of unseen eyes watching from the darkness, and adventurers must uncover the source before the town is overrun.

### **The Order of the Hags**

A malevolent coven has cursed one of the party members, weaving dark magic into their very being. As the affliction worsens, the adventurers must track down the Order of the Hags and break the curse—before their companion is lost to corruption.

### **The Pollution of Petrichor**

Once a land of purity, Petrichor is now tainted. Oozes seep from the earth like festering wounds, a living manifestation of unchecked pollution. As these slimes grow in strength and intelligence, they threaten to consume everything. Is this a natural consequence, or is something more sinister at work?

### **Signs of The Patron**

A town teeters on the brink of madness as fiends emerge from the shadows, whispering riddles of doom and salvation. The appearance of these infernal beings hints at something greater—an ancient Patron, forgotten but not gone. The adventurers must decipher the signs before the town collapses into lunacy.

### **The Lost Technology of the Fairies** _(Robots in an Ancient Facility)_

Deep within a forgotten ruin, an ancient civilization’s legacy slumbers. Unlike the enchanted artifacts of old, this relic of the Fairies is not of flesh or magic—but of metal and code. Who created these machines, and why were they abandoned? What secrets lie within the labyrinthine corridors of this lost facility?

## **The Discovered Southwest Continent of Caerne**

### **DM: Human Solarpunk City**

Caerne stands as a beacon of human ingenuity, a sprawling solarpunk metropolis where technology and nature coexist in harmony. But the newly discovered continent harbors more than just progress—mysteries linger beneath its vibrant utopia, whispering of forgotten forces and hidden conflicts.

## **Scaled Monsters of the West**

### **DM: The Bakun, The Ship-Devouring Water Dragon**

The western seas tremble under the wrath of the Bakun, a legendary water dragon feared by sailors and kings alike. With each passing storm, entire fleets vanish beneath the waves, their wreckage swallowed by the abyss. Some say the Bakun is a force of nature; others claim it is an ancient guardian, punishing those who trespass on forbidden waters.

## **The Hidden Cloud God**

As a child, KK believed the clouds darkened because someone sat upon them—a celestial deity watching the world below. Now, myths resurface of the Hidden Cloud God, an unseen force capable of shaping the heavens.

### **I Miss the Rain**

Rain is more than just water from the sky—it is the manifestation of a love long forbidden. A celestial being, imprisoned in the sky, weeps for their beloved trapped within the earth. Each storm is their sorrow, a fleeting reunion of sky and land.

### **Reverse Rain**

When the Cloud God exerts their will, rain does not fall—it ascends. Rivers defy gravity, droplets rise toward the heavens, and the natural order unravels. In these rare moments, the world glimpses the power of the divine.

## **Family of Liches**

The lich’s secret is not that he refuses to die—but that he is sustained by the generations after him. Each descendant must bear the burden of preserving the Grandfather’s unholy existence. What began as a noble effort to protect their lineage has long since become an abomination.

## **Scarred Friend**

An old, battle-hardened barbarian, his body etched with notches—each a mark of a fallen foe. The party watches in awe as he single-handedly carves through a horde of kobolds, his blade never resting.

Yet, they only realize the true meaning of his scars when one of their own falls in battle. As the warrior carves a new notch into his flesh, they understand—each mark is not for his enemies, but for his lost comrades.

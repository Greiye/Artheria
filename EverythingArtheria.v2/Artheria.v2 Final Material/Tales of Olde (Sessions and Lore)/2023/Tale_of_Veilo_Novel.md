# The Tale of Veilo 

## Session 1

### Veilo, the Mercenary

Up in the Northern Mountains of Caerne, Veilo—a lone soldier in beige armor—climbs rugged terrain under the harsh autumn sun. Around him, glossy purple stones, polished by ancient volcanic activity, mark the path. His flail and shield warm in the emerging light as sweat trickles down his tan, olive skin and into his thick beard. Regret surfaces momentarily—he wished he had purchased a donkey to bear the weight of his gear—but he had doubted a beast’s endurance in this unfamiliar, perilous land.

The morning dew has vanished, and with it the fog, revealing sparse, stunted trees. This harsh region, home to beasts far larger and more ferocious than man, demands caution. Veilo is silently thankful he spared the poor donkey from a gruesome fate at the jaws of a predatory winged creature.

Pausing on a large boulder, Veilo checks his brass pocket watch—four hours since his departure. As the watch flicks open, a family photograph emerges: himself beside a woman with eyes like his, a man, and five small faces below. His thoughts linger on his tsundere, yet caring, sister—her stern kindness a memory that now stings with loss. With a heavy heart, he closes the watch and resumes his journey, the silhouette of a grand castle now emerging in the distance.

---

At the castle’s towering 15-foot portcullis, Veilo meets a fellow warrior. Leaning casually against the gate, the seasoned soldier—later revealed as Sir Luka—observes him with a gaze honed by battle.

**Veilo:** “Hello, Friend. Where are we?”

**Sir Luka:**  
“… You must be who the crown sent for. We do not receive many visitors. This new kingdom’s trail is too treacherous for random passersby.”

After a brief knock, a soldier in a sun visor opens the portcullis slowly. Sir Luka continues:

**Sir Luka:**  
“Welcome to the Breitling Kingdom. The King has been expecting you, Veilo. Come.”

Inside, the castle’s high ceiling reinforces the king’s design—a deliberate choice to make his presence seem larger than life. As they proceed down a raw, half-finished corridor, Sir Luka explains:

**Sir Luka:**  
“I am Sir Luka, Veilo. Your reputation in battle precedes you. Your armor bears no borrowed scars; it is fitted for you, forged from your own victories. Forgive my candor—old courtmen are plenty, but few speak with honesty.”

**Veilo:**  
“Oh, friend, your words do honor the mind as much as the sword. I’ve heard tales of a kingdom where all can read. Is it so?”

**Sir Luka:** _(smirking)_  
“That is true.”

They soon reach a grand set of wooden doors leading to the throne room. A herald proclaims:

**Crier:**  
“Presenting Sir Luka and Veilo! King Breitling receives you!”

Before them, seated upon an imposing throne, is King Breitling—a giant of a man, clad in a silk purple cloak with white fur trim, his short hair neat and his presence awe-inspiring. To his left, a man with a rapier nods curtly; in the king’s right hand rests a claymore with a blade of transparent crystal and a glowing green core—an object Veilo sees for the first time.

The king’s gaze sweeps over Veilo, and with a voice as deep as the trembling earth, he commands:

**King Breitling:**  
“Rise, Soldier.”

Veilo complies, bowing deeply:

**Veilo:**  
“King Breitling, sir. My King.”

King Breitling strides among his court, the castle floor quaking with each step as he speaks of past glories—the Battle of the Horizon—and presents a daunting truth:

**King Breitling:**  
“The Black Bay Kingdom, ruled by Deville, has challenged us. They boasted, ‘If I, King Breitling, ruled the sky, then Black Bay would rule land and sea.’ Such words are now heavy with the weight of battle and loss. I am too small a man to fight them alone… But you, Veilo, possess valor, intelligence, and the strength of character we need. Will you join me?”

**Veilo:**  
“I will, my King.”

“Nothing more,"
"Nothing less.”,
the king replies with a final tone.

---

Abruptly, a disorienting call—“Wake up, Veilo!”—jolts the soldier from what appears to be a visionary slumber. Flames, clashing swords, and the roar of dragons fill his blurred vision. His body aches, his weapon arm in pain, and his flail is nowhere to be seen.

Struggling to focus, he hears a familiar voice:

**Veilo:**  
“Who are you, Habibi? Where am I? What happened?”

A man kneeling beside him answers:

**Teofilo:**  
“It is I, Teofilo—King Breitling’s right hand. I called for you, knowing your worth. Do you remember?”

Gradually, fragments return. Veilo recalls the grand throne room, now ravaged: the throne lies in ruin, shattered stone and broken wood scattered across gaping holes in the castle floor—tunnels carved by desperate men or lurking enemies.

**Veilo:**  
“Gone? Our King… Do you mean
... he is dead?”

Teofilo’s silence confirms the grim truth. 

### Fall of the Giant King
With a steely focus, Veilo demands:

**Veilo:**  
“...Show me the king’s body.”

The very ground begins to rumble—not from the clash of battle, but from the stir of something monstrous beneath. A massive creature, its form hinted by a shark-like fin slicing through the debris, surges toward Teofilo. Acting on instinct, Veilo executes a swift drop kick, sending Teofilo clear by ten feet.

The beast—huge, chitin-armored, and with a maw capable of swallowing a man whole—circles back. It slashes at Veilo; though his shield absorbs a brutal gash along his right arm, the strike leaves a deep scar. In a titanic exchange, a fragment of the creature’s chitin is torn away from its right eye, and blood flows freely.

Amid the chaos, Teofilo retrieves a weapon—a simple rod, bestowed by the fallen King as an honorary gift to new members of the Jagged Edge Knights.

**Teofilo:**  
“Veilo, a gift from Breitling himself. Activate it.”

**Veilo:**  
“A rod? What is this?”

**Teofilo:**  
“Use it.”

Veilo grasps the rod and gives it a firm squeeze. At its tip, a crystal mass shaped like an eight-sided octahedron detaches and glows with a faint green light. It is a Dragon glass flail. With his rapier-wielding comrade in stance, Teofilo declares:

**Teofilo:**  
“You are re-armed. Now, let us end this.”

Together, the two kingsmen confront the monstrous bulette-like creature amid the raging fires, the cacophony of clashing swords, and the roar of dragons. They fight not only for survival, but in the name of a fallen king and the promise of a kingdom reborn.

### A Calm Morning

The first light of dawn crept through the narrow window of Veilo’s room, but habit had roused him long before. An ingrained paranoia, a shadow from battles past, manifested in the ritual of pushing the heavy bed and even the thin mattress against the door

. Only then, his makeshift barricade secure, would he truly begin his day. His morning routine was a testament to discipline honed over years of conflict. A series of stretches, push-ups, and surprisingly fluid, almost gymnastic movements limbered his seasoned frame. Then came the familiar comfort of coffee. Not the simple brew of common folk, but a more intricate process involving a mystical device, a bowl filled with sand that heated with unseen energy. Whispering softly into a strange contraption with numerous buttons, he finally prepared his coffee in a long-handled vessel, the rich aroma filling the sparse room.

As he sipped his coffee, watching the sunrise paint the amethyst peaks beyond his window, Veilo’s thoughts drifted, as they often did, to the vagaries of life.

He jotted down notes on a piece of parchment, a habit that helped him process the unfamiliar world around him. His bag, always within reach, held the familiar weight of his weapons and dungeoneering equipment, the comforting tools of his trade. Today, he also took his piton hammer, a smaller, more discreet weapon. Leaving his room, Veilo couldn’t shake the feeling of being watched. The guards seemed to eye him with a peculiar intensity, and hushed conversations about “outsiders” reached his ears. It was clear that not all locals welcomed those who weren’t born under the shadow of Amethyst Peak. Even a fleeting conversation with the receptionist, whose name he made a mental note to remember, felt charged with unspoken questions. Old habits die hard; he found himself unconsciously touching his beard as he spoke.

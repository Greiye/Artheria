### 🌀 Overview

Aralon is the heart of **Soul** in Artheria’s mind/body/soul triad. Known for its harmony with nature and deep artistic roots, Aralon thrives on emotional truth, tradition, and expression. Where Ranlean seeks progress and Amethyst Peak pursues strength, Aralon reminds the world of peace, presence, and purpose.

---

### 🌧️ Geography & Environment

- **Lush greenery**, **rolling rain-fed hills**, and **salt flats** dominate the landscape.
    
- Frequent **gentle rainfall**, with surprise showers—locals wear stylish **raincoats and wide hats** as everyday attire.
    
- **Hanging gardens**—inspired by Babylon—adorn rooftops, bridges, and temples.
    
- **Clean air and vibrant flora** foster a calm, dreamlike atmosphere.
    

---

### 🎨 Cultural Identity

- Aralon is **the most creative region** in Artheria.
    
- Home to **most Prismari graduates** (artistic magic college).
    
- Towns are dotted with **art exhibits**, **sculptures**, **mosaic walkways**, and **open-air theatres**.
    
- Music, dance, and food are vital parts of daily life.
    
- Residents value **peace, reflection, and soulfulness** over power or progress.
    

---

### 🍽️ Culture & Traditions

- Aralon is famous for **the best food and drink in Artheria**, seasoned with rare salts from its **salt flats**.
    
- Home to the **biggest and best bar**—a neutral meeting ground for pilgrims, exiles, lovers, and poets.
    
- Citizens embrace a simple, fulfilling life rooted in community and emotion.
    
- Art is used not just for beauty, but for **memory**, **mourning**, and **healing**.
    

---

### 🌱 Religion & Spirituality

- **Worship of Petrichor**, the god of agriculture and rainfall, is common.
    
- The region emphasizes **soulful living**, **gratitude**, and **preservation of peace**.
    
- Aralon serves as a **neutral pilgrimage site** for citizens across Artheria—its temples welcome all.
    
- The guiding principle:
    
    > _“Peace is the greatest reward—acknowledge and cherish it.”_
    

---

### 🧑‍🎨 Society

- Artists, philosophers, and wanderers thrive here.
    
- **Nonviolent problem-solving** is a cultural expectation—duels are replaced with debates, art-offs, or soul games.
    
- **Environmentally conscious**: hanging gardens filter air and grow rare fruits not found in the highlands or cities.
    

---

### 🪧 Architecture & Aesthetic

- Homes are made of **pale stone**, **natural wood**, and **living walls of plants**.
    
- Rain chains and terrace gutters funnel water into **community gardens**.
    
- City centers feature **open plazas**, **statue-lined roads**, and **salt-sculpture festivals**.
    

---

### ⚖️ Contrasts & Conflicts

- **Seen as passive** by Amethyst Peak and **naïve** by Ranlean.
    
- In truth, Aralon’s strength lies in its **emotional resilience**, **cultural preservation**, and **community cohesion**.
    
- Its **neutral stance in political conflict** makes it both a sanctuary and a target.
    

---

### 🧭 Key Town: **Aralon (Capital)**

- Known for its artists, creatives, and philosophers.
    
- Frequented by travelers from other regions seeking peace, inspiration, or retreat.
    
- Major landmarks:
    
    - **The Hanging Gardens**
        
    - **The Chapel of the First** (worship of Petrichor)
        
    - **The Salt Bazaar** (famous for spices, pigments, and rare herbs)
        
    - **Granny Smith’s Cottage**, **Magic Greer’s Atelier**, and **Father Padre’s Chapel**

## Old Lore

# Aralon
A small farming Town, Aralon is known to be the hub of creatives and artists, often in the Hanging Gardens. Thanks to the numerous salt flats close to the town, Aralon can provide everyone with quality food products to all neighboring settlements. Citizens are always wearing raincoats and hats in the event of sudden rain.

On the outskirts of town is a **farmer’s market**, where you can meet Granny Smith and her pets, Magic Greer and all his magical wares. Be sure to visit Father Padre who runs a Chapel of the First.

Petrichor is a popular god of Agriculture in this part of the Hymlie Principality.

## Aralon’s Goal:
A Peaceful life is its own reward. Everyone has a right to it. Acknowledge it, cherish it, for when it is gone in the storm of life, it is all that you wish you had.
#### Elyon, the Old Name
Underneath Aralon is an old mining settlement. It was active thousands of years ago, until the inhabitants were massacred.

Since then, the name slowly changed to Aralon.

Elyon used to have 2 distinct groups, the high elves that guarded the entrances above ground and the dark elves that mined underneath. Some say that even before then, It used to be a Halfling mine, until that era was killed off. Their artifacts were sold off, and Dark elves moved in. Although not as quick as the halflings, mining was not a monopoly done only by halflings. They picked up where the left off.

The partnership was necessary so that the town could function as it once did. At first the relationship was uneasy, but later stabilized.

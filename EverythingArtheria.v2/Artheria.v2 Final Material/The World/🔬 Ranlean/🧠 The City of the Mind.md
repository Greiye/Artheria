### 🏙️ Overview

Ranlean is the most progressive and intellectually advanced region in Artheria. It represents the **Mind** in the triad of Mind, Body, and Soul. The city is known for its devotion to knowledge, innovation, and structured societal systems—but beneath its brilliance lies deep social inequality and subtle corruption.

---

### 🌊 Origins

- **Founded by**: **Vellichor**, god of the Ephemerals (knowledge, memory, and discovery).
    
- **Location**: Built atop lands rich in **mercury lakes**, which became the foundation for its technological and magical advancements.
    
- **Symbol**: The **Mercury Flame** – a glass orb filled with quicksilver, burning with internal light.
    

---

### 💡 Key Features

- **Largest Library in Artheria**, complete with a **planetarium** inspired by Avatar’s Wan Shi Tong’s archive.
    
- **Highly developed infrastructure**:
    
    - Organized police force (RPD – Ranlean Police Department)
        
    - Hospital system
        
    - Banking and storage systems
        
    - Advanced schools and labs
        
- **Lighting Tech**: Powered by mercury, the city is **well-lit** day and night, with **lightbulbs** lining every street.
    
- **Academic Hub**: Home to the **most Strixhaven graduates** in Artheria, especially from **Silverquill** and **Quandrix** colleges.
    

---

### 🧬 Society

- **Cultural Value**: Intelligence, invention, and academic achievement.
    
- **Dress & Architecture**: Scholars' robes, brass and glass fixtures, clean stone roads, and technomagical machinery.
    
- **Technology**: The most advanced city in Artheria; mercury fuels many magical devices.
    

---

### 🧍 Unmorrowed and Social Tension

- **The Unmorrowed**: Citizens born with **no magical ability**, seen as having “no place in tomorrow.”
    
    - Often relegated to second-class status.
        
    - Podrigo and his followers work to **advance science without magic**.
        
- **Spellcasters** dominate positions of power and social respect.
    
- A growing **scientific counterculture** is rising—one that questions magic’s monopoly on progress.
    

---

### ⚖️ Corruption

Despite its progressive exterior, Ranlean is **rife with quiet corruption**:

- **Ripley Darkwater**: High-profile lawyer who covers up key scandals like the Rot Plague. Father of **Dickie Darkwater**, a Silverquill student unaware of his father’s corruption.
    
- **Darkwater Influence**: Ivan (judge), Roman (bailiff), Evie and Dante (jury bribers) orchestrated Podrigo’s acquittal.
    
- The **Ranlean elite protect their own**, especially those who preserve the magical status quo.
    

---

### 🧪 The Rot Wizard Incident

- **Dr. Aldos Podrigo**, aka the **Rot Wizard**, was once a celebrated Unmorrowed scientist.
    
- Created the **Rot Plague**, which targets and decays mana in spellcasters.
    
- Experimented on Konnor, Kori, and Rok—leading to death, paralysis, and the loss of pseudomagic.
    
- **Found innocent** in a corrupted court trial.
    
- Mocked the victims with the **Konnor Grant**, a scholarship for "Unmorrowed with potential."
    

---

### 🔦 City Vibes

- **Atmosphere**: Bright, polished, and forward-looking—yet impersonal and cold.
    
- **Philosophy**: “The mind is a concealed blade.”
    
- **Color Palette**: Mercury silver, glass blue, parchment beige, and arcane green.

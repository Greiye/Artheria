# _The Rot Wizard_

**Race/Class**: Unmorrowed (non-magical), Alchemist  
**Public Role**: Revered Ranlean scientist  
**Secret Identity**: Creator of the Rot Plague  
**Inspired by**: Lex Luthor / Golgari-style necromancer

---

### 🧥 Public Persona

Dr. Podrigo is seen as one of the **most respected scientists in Ranlean**, a city that prides itself on knowledge, progress, and magical innovation. He contributed to major breakthroughs in medicine and mana engineering. His signature leather gear, which covers him head-to-toe, is thought to be for protection from infection. He is viewed as eccentric but harmless.

He lives alone in a **mushroom-covered forest lab** just outside Ranlean, occasionally waving to passersby and helping lost travelers. Most assume he’s simply a reclusive genius.

---

### 🧬 Secret Truth

Podrigo is **Unmorrowed**—a term describing those born without a trace of magic, in a society where magic defines value. Unmorrowed are seen as having “no place in tomorrow.” This lifelong rejection fostered his **hatred for magic users**, especially spellcasters born with power.

Driven by bitterness and intellect, Podrigo:

- Assembled a secret group of Unmorrowed scientists to **advance science beyond magic**.
    
- Captured and experimented on **mana-craving desmodhamps**, discovering how to isolate and manipulate spellcaster mana.
    
- Created the **Rot Plague**, a disease that targets spellcasters by **decaying their mana** and corrupting their magical abilities.
    
- Used the **soul of Konnor**, an Unmorrowed child infected by the desmodhamp, to finalize the plague. The soul exchange turned Konnor into a **mindless berserker** with no hunger for mana, which Podrigo considered “better.”
    

---

### 🧫 Kori, Konnor, and the Catalyst

- **Kori and Konnor** were sent to the lab after Konnor was infected and turned feral when healed by a cleric.
    
- On their way to Podrigo’s lab, Kori manifested **pseudomagic** in a moment of desperation, knocking out two guards.
    
- Podrigo gave her **concentrated mana** to suppress her hunger—before revealing he had already used Konnor’s soul to create the Rot Plague.
    
- This triggered a fight. **Rok was paralyzed**, and **Kori lost the ability to cast pseudomagic**. Konnor, now soulless and uncontrollable, locked Kori and Rok in the lab.
    

Podrigo’s final words to Kori:

> _“It’s a shame you won’t be able to see my reign as the Rot Wizard.”_

---

### ⚖️ The Trial

Podrigo was taken to court in Ranlean for:

- Creating the Rot Plague
    
- Killing Konnor
    
- Paralyzing Rok
    
- Robbing Kori of her magic
    

Despite overwhelming evidence, he was found **innocent** due to his **connections to the Darkwaters**:

- **Judge**: Ivan Darkwater
    
- **Defense Lawyer**: Ripley Darkwater
    
- **Bailiff**: Roman Darkwater
    
- **Jury**: Bribed by the Darkwater Twins, Evie and Dante
    

To mock Kori further, Podrigo **created a scholarship** in Konnor’s name called **The Konnor Grant**, claiming it would support other “Unmorrowed with potential.”

---

### 🧠 Philosophy

Podrigo believes:

- Magic is an **unearned privilege**.
    
- Souls are **fuel**, not sacred.
    
- Science should **replace** magic, not work alongside it.
    
- The world’s obsession with spellcasting is a **delusion**—and only the Unmorrowed can see the truth.
    

Quotes:

> _“Magic is a lie whispered by the privileged to make the rest of us believe we are weak.”_  
> _“The soul is not a sacred thing. It is fuel.”_  
> _“If I had committed such acts, Ranlean would no longer be standing.”_

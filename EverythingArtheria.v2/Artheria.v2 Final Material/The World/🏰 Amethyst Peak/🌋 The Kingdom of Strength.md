### 🛡️ Overview

Amethyst Peak represents the **Body** in the triad of Artheria. It is a proud, powerful military kingdom built atop the world’s tallest volcano. The region is steeped in ancient bloodlines, brutal trials, and divine combat. Every stone whispers the names of warriors past, and the skies burn with dragonfire.

---

### 🔥 Geography & Environment

- **Dominated by volcanic terrain**: lava flows, sulfur vents, ash plains.
    
- Home to **volcano-forged caverns** that serve as **tombs, dungeons, or treasure chambers**—relics of ancient battles.
    
- **Sulfur in the air** gives the region its sharp scent and fiery skies.
    
- **Crimson and gold hues** dominate the landscape.
    

---

### 🐉 Key Features

- **Military Academy**: The finest warrior school in Artheria. Known for unarmed combat, weapon mastery, and aerial dragon-mounted tactics.
    
- **Dragonkin Presence**: Dragons are respected and feared. Some serve the kingdom; others dwell wild in the deep peaks.
    
- **Dragonglass Weapons**: Forged from the molten glass left behind by dragons. Magical and ancient—each weapon is unique and often named.
    

---

### ⚔️ Cultural Values

- **Strength through adversity** (Lachesism).
    
- Legacy and bloodline define status.
    
- Combat as both an art and a rite of passage.
    
- **The Godkin**: Descendants of divine rulers like King Tristen Victorie and King Anarch.
    

---

### 👑 Royal Lineage & Politics

- **Founded by**: **Anarch**, deity of Lachesism, who attempted to tame dragons and harness their magic.
    
- **Current Monarch**: **King Teofilo Blackthorn** (Filo the Amereoux) — a bardic blade dancer and inspiring, charismatic leader.
    
- **Past Rulers**:
    
    - **King Tristen Victorie** (“The Mourning Glory”) – legendary undefeated warrior, whose grief following his family's assassination ended his reign.
        
    - **King Malrec Lazay** (“The Hand That Feeds”) – tried to use dragons to destroy dissenters and was overthrown.
        
    - **King Bjorn Breitling** (“The Bulwark Flame”) – fell during the Battle for the Horizon.
        

---

### ⚒️ The Jagged Edge Knights

- Elite defenders of the kingdom.
    
- Each knight wields a unique **magical dragonglass weapon**.
    
- Founded by **King Teofilo**.
    
- **Luka Celestine**, a recent member, was killed during the “Scorched Earth” betrayal by **Biliran**, Filo’s grandson.
    

---

### 🧬 The Godkin

- The royal bloodline descended from Anarch is considered divine.
    
- Notable members:
    
    - **Bon Victorie** – A Desert Storm Barbarian and Amethyst Peak’s golden boy, favored by the court.
        
    - **Cela Victorie** – His overlooked twin sister, a brilliant fighter who later betrays the kingdom.
        
    - **Lancia** – Half-dragon daughter of Lance Victorie and a potential future queen.
        
    - **Belial & Biliran Blackthorn** – Ruthless, destructive warriors who follow Anarch’s ideals of strength.
        

---

### 💀 Cultural Themes

- **Honor through sacrifice**.
    
- **Warrior funerals** held at the volcano rim—ashes returned to the fire.
    
- **Body-focused rituals**: tattoos, scars, and ceremonial piercings tell stories of battles won.
    

---

### 🧱 Architecture & Life

- **Stone citadels**, **obsidian towers**, and **dragon-perch spires** line the mountains.
    
- Interiors built for function, sparring, and strategy.
    
- Common citizens live in fortified homes near sulfur vents, with storm shelters for dragon activity.
    

---

### ⚖️ Tone & Contrasts

- **Visceral** and **heroic**.
    
- Ancient pride clashes with **modern vulnerability**—dragons are fewer, bloodlines thinner, and past glories fading.
    
- **Ideal vs. Reality**: Bon is idolized for strength, but Cela is passed over due to court prejudice. Filo fights to maintain morale as legacy collapses around him.

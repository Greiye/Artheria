### 🛡️ **Titles & Role**

- **Squire of Sir Luka Celestine**
    
- Known affectionately as **“Petite Arthur”** by King Teofilo
    
- Companion of the falcon familiar **Millenia**
    

---

### 🧑‍🎓 **Overview**

Arthur Aiden is the loyal, bright-eyed squire to **Sir Luka Celestine**, trained in the Crucible Hall beneath Castle Blackthorn. Though young and small—both in stature and presence—Arthur’s **keen intuition, quiet bravery, and absolute loyalty** made him an indispensable partner to Luka.

He serves as a subtle homage to the nickname **“Arturito”** (a common mishearing of R2-D2 in some Spanish-speaking communities), and mirrors the dynamic of R2-D2 to Luke Skywalker. His relationship with Luka was part student-teacher, part protector-ward, and part **unspoken family**.

---

### 🪶 **Character Traits**

- Highly observant and emotionally intelligent
    
- Soft-spoken, deferential to authority, but not cowardly
    
- Anxious under pressure but surprisingly **resilient in crisis**
    
- Protective of Millenia, who often scouts and warns ahead for him and Luka
    

---

### 🕊️ **Falcon Familiar: Millenia**

Arthur’s familiar is a sleek gray falcon named **Millenia**, magically bonded to him. She serves as his eyes, scout, and emotional anchor.

Millenia is now the **only confirmed witness** to Luka’s death at the hands of Biliran Blackthorn. She escaped during the chaos and returned to report the betrayal to **Belial**, who twisted her message to suit his aims.

Arthur’s bond with Millenia means she may return to him, carrying guilt, memories, or even **magical trauma**.

---

### ⚔️ **Scorched Earth – The Turning Point**

Arthur accompanied Luka and Biliran into the sulfur caverns of **Pele’s Playground** to find the portal to Strixhaven. During the journey:

- Arthur noticed tension between Luka and Biliran, and tried to mediate.
    
- He attempted spells like _Spiritual Weapon_ and _Shield of Faith_, but was outmatched by the hostile environment and Biliran’s aggression.
    
- At one point, Biliran **threw Arthur into the portal** in a moment of rage or tactical dismissal.
    
- **Whether Arthur survived, where he landed, or if he ever came back is currently unknown.**
    

Many believe he is lost. Others suspect he is hiding—**out of trauma, shame, or fear.**

---

### 🪞 **Current Status: Missing**

- As far as the court knows, Arthur was “lost” during the mission.
    
- No body was recovered.
    
- **King Filo still calls him “Petite Arthur” in passing**, sometimes without realizing.
    
- **Millenia’s return** and lingering behavior suggest Arthur is alive—but changed.

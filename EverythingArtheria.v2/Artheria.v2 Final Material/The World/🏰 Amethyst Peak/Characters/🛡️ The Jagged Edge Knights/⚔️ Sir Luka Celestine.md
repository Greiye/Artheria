### 🛡️ **Titles & Roles**

- **Jagged Edge Knight of Amethyst Peak**
    
- **Bearer of the Mourning Blade**
    
- **Knight-Philosopher of the Crucible Hall**
    
- Known in whispers as _The Silent Flame_
    

---

### 📖 **Overview**

Sir Luka Celestine is—**or was**—the most respected of the **Jagged Edge Knights**, the order of elite warrior-philosophers founded by King Filo to temper power with restraint.

Known for his introspective nature and elegant swordsmanship, Luka embodied the code of the Edge: justice without judgment, balance before blade, and grief as a teacher. He was more than a knight—he was **a symbol**, a north star for an order that demanded clarity in chaos.

---

### ⚔️ **The Mourning Blade**

After the death of King Bjorn Breitling, the **Mourning Blade**, a green dragon glass greatsword forged in sorrow, passed to Luka—not through blood, but **through trust**.

- In his hands, the blade became a thing of **stillness, not spectacle**.
    
- Its green glow pulsed softly in the presence of magic and emotional unrest.
    
- It was said the blade refused to sing unless Luka truly believed it should.
    

> _“A blade’s sharpness is not in its edge, but in its purpose.”_

---

### 🔍 **Mentorship & Duty**

Luka trained younger knights and squires in the **Crucible Hall** beneath Castle Blackthorn. His most faithful student was **Arthur Aiden**, a squire with a strong sense of justice and a falcon familiar named **Millenia**.

He was King Filo’s quiet confidant, the hand that struck when words failed. Unlike the kings before him, Filo did not need a brute enforcer—he needed a **moral compass** with a blade. Luka was that man.

---

### 🔥 **The Betrayal at Pele’s Playground** _(Scorched Earth)_

Luka was sent to escort **Biliran Blackthorn**, Filo’s grandson, and Arthur to the sulfur caverns where a portal to Strixhaven was hidden. But **Biliran carried secret orders from his father, Belial**—to kill the knight and the heir, and to claim the Mourning Blade.

What happened next remains known only to a **single witness**: Millenia, the squire’s falcon.

- Luka confronted Biliran after growing suspicious of his intent.
    
- The two dueled, and though Luka wounded Biliran, **he was ultimately cast into the lava**.
    
- His blade changed color—**from tranquil green to a violent red**—and was taken by Biliran.
    

> Luka’s final words were said to be:  
> _“I wish you were on our side.”_

---

### ⚠️ **Status: Unconfirmed**

Though Millenia has returned to Belial and the truth is known in certain dark corners, **King Filo has not yet learned of Luka’s death**.

To the court, **Luka is still “on mission.”**  
To the Jagged Edge Knights, his absence is deeply troubling.  
To Arthur Aiden—missing and presumed traumatized—his silence speaks volumes.

> Some believe the blade lives still.  
> Others whisper Luka may yet rise—**dragonglass does not yield so easily to flame.**

---

### 🕯️ **Legacy in Tension**

In the Crucible Hall, Luka’s training chamber remains untouched. His candle, once lit each morning for meditation, now sits cold.

> _“We do not name the dead until the sword returns.”_  
> —Knight Adage, inscribed in the Crucible Hall

His falcon, Millenia, is rumored to be circling the Black Bay coastlines. Watching. Waiting.

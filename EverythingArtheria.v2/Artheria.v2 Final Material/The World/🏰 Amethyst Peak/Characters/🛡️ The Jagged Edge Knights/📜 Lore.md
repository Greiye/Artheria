_“Tempered by grief. Sharpened by restraint.”_

Founded by **King Teofilo Blackthorn**, the Jagged Edge Knights are an elite order of warrior-philosophers who serve not the crown, but the **moral conscience of the realm**. Born in the wake of tragedy and hubris, they were created to prevent the mistakes of the kings before Filo—to ensure that **power never outruns wisdom**.

Where soldiers follow orders, the Jagged Edge Knights follow **principle**.

---

### 🧭 **The Creed of the Edge**

Every knight learns three foundational tenets:

1. **Balance Before Blade**  
    _“Strike only when no song can save.”_  
    A reminder that violence is never the first path—only the last.
    
2. **Justice Without Judgment**  
    _“We do not pass sentence. We illuminate truth.”_  
    Knights do not act as executioners, but as **witnesses and shields** to those the law might forget.
    
3. **Grief Is a Teacher**  
    _“Pain sharpens the edge—but we do not let it guide the hand.”_  
    Emotion is not weakness, but it must be mastered, not indulged.
    

---

### 🐉 **Dragon Glass Weapons**

Each Jagged Edge Knight carries a **weapon forged from magical dragon glass**, a volatile volcanic crystal that responds to emotion, memory, and identity. No two weapons are the same, and no weapon can be wielded by more than one Knight unless **the blade accepts them**.

- Luka Celestine's blade glowed **green-blue**, reflecting clarity and awareness.
    
- After his death, it turned **red**, reacting to **Biliran’s internal conflict and volcanic rage**.
    

The forging of the weapon is a rite of passage. A Knight must face the **Flame of Reflection**, a magical crucible where the blade takes form based on the Knight’s inner truth. Some blades manifest instantly. Others... never form at all.

---

### 🏛️ **The Crucible Hall**

Located beneath Castle Blackthorn, in a chamber carved into the volcano itself, the Crucible Hall is the Jagged Edge sanctuary and training ground. It is a place of **quiet, meditation, and movement**, filled with volcanic steam, carved obsidian, and ambient magical wards that respond to emotion.

At its center is a dais where the **Mourning Blade** once rested. After Luka’s death, the dais remains empty—**a symbol of the order’s fracture**.

---

### 👥 **Ranks Within the Order**

1. **The Edgebound** – Full knights with their bonded blade. Trusted to act autonomously.
    
2. **Squires of the Flame** – Young initiates who train under an Edgebound. Not yet bonded to a blade.
    
3. **The Forged** – Masters of philosophy and swordwork who have mentored at least three Knights to full oath. Often serve as advisors to rulers.
    
4. **The Severed** – Former Knights who either lost their blade or failed their trial. Some live in exile, others seek redemption.
    

---

### 🕊️ **Notable Figures**

- **King Teofilo Blackthorn** – Founder and philosophical father of the Order. His blade dancing style became the foundation of Jagged Edge combat.
    
- **Sir Luka Celestine** – Beloved Knight, bearer of the Mourning Blade, and martyr of the _Scorched Earth_ betrayal.
    
- **Arthur Aiden** – Squire and sole survivor of Luka’s final mission. His falcon familiar, **Millenia**, is now treated as a sacred witness.
    
- **The Silent Blade** – A mysterious Knight who took a vow of silence and now protects the Crucible Hall. Rumored to have failed their trial… or been exiled by choice.
    

---

### 🌋 **Current State of the Order**

Since Luka’s death, the Order has been in quiet disarray. Filo maintains the **appearance** of control, but whispers grow:

- That the Knights are divided between **traditionalists** and **radicals**.
    
- That a **new blade** has begun to form in the Crucible—**and it burns red**.
    
- That **Biliran’s bond to Luka’s blade** may either destroy or redeem the Order… depending on whether he follows fire or restraint.

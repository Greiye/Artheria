---

### 🛡️ **The Rise of the Interim Flame**

After the death of **King Tristen Victorie**, who perished from grief after the assassination of his family, the realm faced a dangerous vacuum. Into that void stepped **Bjorn Breitling**, Tristen’s High Marshal and most trusted commander.

Though not of divine blood, Bjorn’s sheer physical presence, tactical brilliance, and unshakable resolve made him the ideal steward. Standing over **twelve feet tall**, he ruled not through legacy, but through **magnitude**.

The ancestral fortress was renamed **Castle Breitling**, and with it, he took up the **Mourning Blade**, the dragon glass greatsword once wielded by Tristen—no longer weeping, but watching.

---

### 🏰 **The Giant King**

Bjorn reshaped the seat of power to reflect his stature. Massive doorways, vaulted halls, and oversized thrones turned **Castle Breitling** into a physical metaphor for dominion.

> “Let the seat of power remind us what power looks like.”  
> —King Bjorn, commissioning the High Portcullis

His rule was one of **discipline, vigilance, and preparation**. While he was not a warm ruler, he was a reliable one—and under his reign, the castle stood firm, the skies remained guarded, and the people endured.

---

### ⚔️ **The Mourning Blade**

Passed to him after Tristen’s death, the Mourning Blade became a symbol of solemn responsibility. Forged from green dragon glass, it had once been a weapon of sorrow. In Bjorn’s hands, it became a standard of **authority without indulgence**.

He drew it only for ritual and law—never for show.

---

### 🛡️ **The Jagged Edge Knights**

Though the **Jagged Edge Knights** had been founded by his right hand, **Teofilo Blackthorn**, during his reign, Bjorn legitimized them through royal endorsement. While others saw them as an idealistic force of warrior-philosophers, Bjorn viewed them as **a necessary conscience** to temper his might.

> “Let them walk beside the sword, not behind it.”  
> —Bjorn, upon officially recognizing the order

Among their number was **Sir Luka Celestine**, to whom the Mourning Blade was later passed.

---

### 🔥 **The Battle for the Horizon** _(A Premonition)_

In a vision witnessed by **Veilo**, a mercenary knight later inducted into the Jagged Edge, the **fall of Castle Breitling** unfolds:

- **Bjorn is confirmed dead**—the throne shattered, the Mourning Blade gone.
    
- Castle Breitling is attacked by **subterranean chitin-covered beasts**, erupting from beneath the throne.
    
- **Teofilo**, now leading the defense, gifts Veilo a dragon glass rod that becomes a flail—his induction into the order.
    
- The battle burns the Breitling name into legend, and the castle is soon renamed **Castle Blackthorn** under the new king: **Teofilo himself**.
    

---

### 🕯️ **Legacy of a Giant**

Bjorn Breitling died not with fanfare, but with stone falling, fire raging, and a realm on the edge.

He left behind no heirs, no verses, no funeral rites.  
Only towering ruins, a changed name, and a memory of a man **too large to be ignored**.

The Mourning Blade would reappear later in the hands of Luka Celestine—until it, too, vanished into lava and myth.

> “A king of silence. A throne of stone. A legacy that cast no shadow, but stood in the way of them all.”  
> —Inscribed beneath his fallen throne

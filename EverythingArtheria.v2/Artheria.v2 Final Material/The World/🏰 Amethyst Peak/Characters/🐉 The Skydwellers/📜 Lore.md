### 🔱 **Divine Lineage**

The Sky Dwellers are the **royal bloodline of Amethyst Peak**, descended from **King Anarch**, the deity of **Lachesism**—the belief that struggle strengthens the soul. Every monarch born of this line is considered a **Godkin**, touched by divine flame and burdened with rulership forged in hardship.

---

### 📜 **The Lineage of Flame**

#### 🔥 **King Anarch** – _The First Flame_

The deity of Lachesism, Anarch tamed the volcano of Amethyst Peak and sought to **harness dragonkind** to test and elevate mortals. Though his ambition was never fulfilled, his bloodline became the ruling flame of the Peak.

---

#### 🖐️ **King Malrec Lazay** – _The Hand That Feeds_

A distant descendant of Anarch, Lazay believed a king should withhold nothing—not even power over life itself. He ruled with open hands… until his people rebelled. In response, he attempted **regicide in reverse**—turning his dragons on the very citizens he had once uplifted. He was **overthrown by Tristen Victorie**.

---

#### ⚔️ **King Tristen Victorie** – _The Mourning Glory

The noble warrior who **defeated Lazay**, Tristen ruled with deep love for his people—but **loved too deeply**. After the **assassination of his family**, he fell into grief and later died of heartbreak. His greatsword, once a weapon of glory, became known as **The Mourning Blade**.

---

#### 💔 **King Bjorn Breitling** – _The Bulwark Flame_

Though not Godkin, Bjorn was named king after Tristen’s death to **stabilize the realm**. A towering giant and bearer of the Mourning Blade, he ruled with silence and discipline until his death in the **Battle for the Horizon**.

---

#### 🎭 **King Teofilo Blackthorn** – _Filo the Amereoux_

The current king, Teofilo is **a bardic ruler**, full of charm and charisma, ruling from **Castle Blackthorn**. Though descended from the divine line, Filo takes a different approach: he **does not chase greatness**, but nurtures beauty, loyalty, and principle. He is founder of the **Jagged Edge Knights** and a lover of diplomacy and blade-dancing. He was once Bjorn’s right hand and now seeks to mend what kings before him broke.

---

### 👑 **Titles & Traits of the Sky Dwellers**

- Known as **Godkin**, their blood is considered sacred.
    
- Born to rule, but often **broken by it**.
    
- Every Sky Dweller reflects a different interpretation of **strength**:
    
    - **Anarch**: Adversity
        
    - **Lazay**: Control
        
    - **Tristen**: Compassion
        
    - **Bjorn**: Discipline
        
    - **Filo**: Inspiration

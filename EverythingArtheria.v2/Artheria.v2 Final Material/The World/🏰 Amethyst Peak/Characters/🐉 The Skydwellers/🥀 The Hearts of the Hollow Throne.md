
There are three crowns buried beneath Amethyst Peak, and none are whole. Their names are etched in myth and marble alike, but they are not remembered for what they ruled—only for what they could not bear.

They are known not merely as kings, but as echoes in the stone.  
**The Hearts of the Hollow Throne.**

> **“Three kings sat the hollow throne.**  
> **One ruled cold, his heart a stone.**  
> **One ruled blind, too far, too near.**  
> **One ruled full, then drowned in fear.**  
> **None could hold what could not be owned—**  
> **The weight of love, the hollow throne.”**

---

### 🖤 **The First Heart – The King Who Cared for No One**

The first to wear the Sky Crown, [[EverythingArtheria.v2/Jason Notes/The Ephemerals of Nepenthe/Characters/Anarch|Anarch]] was no mere king—he was divine. Born of starlight and storm, he descended the volcano not to shepherd the people, but to **conquer the dragons**. He believed emotion was weakness, that empathy dulled the blade of rule.

Anarch forged a kingdom in his image: **strong, cold, and beautiful**—like obsidian. He tamed wildfires and caged magic, but **never offered warmth** to the people beneath him.  
He ruled with fire and **felt no flame**.

When Anarch vanished into the veil, he left behind a throne untouched by love—and thus, hollow.

---

### 💀 **The Second Heart – The King Who Cared Too Little** 

Where Anarch ruled by distance, [[King Malrec Lazay]] ruled by disdain. He **inherited fire** and twisted it into shadow. He wore his crown like armor, his people like pawns. His heart was not cold, but **empty**, a vessel filled only by hunger for control.

He became known as **"The Hand That Feeds"**, for he doled out just enough to keep the kingdom dependent, starved of affection yet addicted to order. But even a starving people know the taste of poison.

> **"And so," the poets say, "they bit the hand that feeds."**

It is said that Lazay **never wept, never knelt, never dreamed.** His reign saw the growth of empire but the rot of its roots. Dragons grew restless. The people grew afraid. He knew power, but **not presence**.

And in time, even fear abandoned him. The crown slid from his brow as easily as silence.

---

### 💔 **The Third Heart – The King Who Cared Too Much** 

[[King Tristen Victorie]] rose in rebellion, sword in hand, grief in his eyes. He defeated Lazay in the **Battle for the Horizon**, and for the first time in generations, **Amethyst Peak had a king who loved his people** more than his legacy.

He walked among soldiers, ate among commoners, and held his family close. Too close.

For while Tristen knew love, he did not know limits. When assassins came for his kin, he blamed himself.  
When his grandchildren, Bon and Cela, were the only ones spared, his heart did not break—it **withered**.  
He gave his heart, and it was **broken**.

He died not by blade or betrayal, but by grief. And so the third crown was buried beneath stone and sorrow.

---

### 🔮 **The Curse of the Hollow Throne**

They say the throne remembers them.  
Three kings, three hearts—none whole.

And now, each ruler who dares sit the Sky Crown must ask themselves:  
**Will I freeze? Will I fade? Will I fall?**

For the Hollow Throne does not demand gold, nor loyalty, nor might.  
It demands **balance**—a heart not untouched by love, but not undone by it.

No such ruler has come.  
Not yet.

### 🔱 **Titles & Epithets**

- _The Victis_ – “Woe to the Conquered”
    
- _The Loving King_
    
- _Mourning Glory_
    
- _The Golden Flame_
    

---

### 📜 **The Rise**

In the final years of **Malrec Lazay’s reign**, the tyrant grew paranoid. Rebellions stirred in the streets of Amethyst Peak, and the people—starved, silenced, and stripped of hope—rose with fire in their voices. Enraged by defiance, **Lazay unleashed dragons upon his own citizens**, scorching entire districts in a brutal act of control.

It was not war. It was **a king attempting to kill his own people** to preserve his crown.

From the ashes rose **Tristen Victorie**, a soldier of noble descent who rallied the defenders of the realm. Armed with his ancestral greatsword—**forged of dragon glass**—he led a final charge on Castle Blackthorne.  
**Malrec Lazay fell not by the will of gods, but by the will of the people—through Tristen’s hand.**

Thus, Tristen claimed the Hollow Throne—not by inheritance, but by justice.

---

### 🛡️ **The Reign**

Tristen ruled with a heart unarmored. He **dined with soldiers**, rebuilt the city from the ruins of tyranny, and poured love into his kingdom the way others poured coin. His strength was not only in steel, but in empathy.

He refused to let the throne define him. He let the people do that instead. And they called him king not because he ruled—but because he **cared**.

---

### 💔 **The Fall**

But love, unchecked, bears its own weight. Tristen’s greatest strength became his greatest sorrow.

In a single night, his family was **assassinated**. His children, spouse, and closest advisors—gone. Only **Bon and Cela**, his twin grandchildren, survived. He shielded them from the world while his own world **collapsed**.

From that moment, his greatsword—**forged of dragon glass**, rare and luminous—was **no longer spoken of by its ancestral name**. It became known as **The Mourning Blade**, for its wielder no longer fought for conquest, but in grief.  
He carried it like a **gravestone** in his hands.

When Tristen died, not in battle but from heartbreak, the Mourning Blade passed to **High Marshal Bjorn Breitling**, a trusted steward of the realm. He wielded it not with emotion, but with **stern discipline**, upholding order in the unstable years that followed.

Only after **Bjorn’s death** did the blade pass once more—this time not to a ruler, but to a guardian.  
It was entrusted to **Sir Luka Celestine**, a Jagged Edge Knight of great promise, marking the blade’s rebirth **as a weapon of conscience**, not power.

---

### ⚰️ **Legacy**

They laid Tristen to rest beneath the mountain he had once saved. His tomb bears a single line:

> _“He gave his heart, and it was broken.”_

His era became known as **Vae Victis**—_Woe to the Conquered_—not for his enemies, but for the king himself, who conquered a tyrant and lost everything.

To this day, when someone rises for love, they say:

> “He walks with Tristen.”  
> And when someone falls from it,  
> “He carries the Mourning Blade.”

### 💃 **The Lovelorn King**

Teofilo Blackthorn, King of Amethyst Peak, is many things: a dancer, a duelist, a ruler, a lover. His reputation precedes him—**a man of effortless charm, romantic entanglements, and the kind of smile that starts revolutions or ends them**.

He’s been seen slipping out of noble balconies at dawn, arriving late to council with lipstains on his collar, and making enemies swoon with a sentence. But behind the charm lies **a mind sharper than his blade**, and a soul carved from the pressure of legacy.

Where most kings sit above their people, Filo **walks among them**—barefoot, laughing, blade trailing behind him like a whisper. They call him a flirt. A fool. A dancer.

But they follow him.

---

### 🛡️ **A Bard Who Moved, Not Sang**

Filo never ruled with anthems or decrees. He ruled with **motion**—a warrior-poet whose martial art, **blade dancing**, became a visual language of elegance, restraint, and danger. Every step is choreography. Every parry a punctuation.

But it is **not the dance that wins hearts**.  
It is **his presence**.

He sees people. Remembers names. Speaks plainly. **His charisma is not just performative—it’s magnetic**, and his leadership stems not from authority, but from belief.

> _“The blade dazzles. But it’s the dancer they trust.”_  
> —Anonymous courtier

---

### 👑 **The Unexpected Flame**

Never meant to rule, Filo was a second son who studied diplomacy and martial grace in Ranlean while others trained for war. When tragedy shattered the line of succession, it was Filo’s voice—**not his bloodline**—that held the realm together.

He took the throne not through conquest, but by commanding attention in a world that had forgotten how to listen.

---

### ⚔️ **Founder of the Jagged Edge Knights**

To prevent the mistakes of the past, Filo founded the **Jagged Edge Knights**, a brotherhood of elite warriors trained to wield **dragon glass weapons**, each forged through ritual, grief, and self-reflection.

These knights, bound not to a crown but to a code, are Filo’s living answer to the Hollow Throne's history of ruin. Their edge is jagged because so is their purpose: to protect the realm **from itself** when necessary.

Filo entrusted **The Mourning Blade** to **Sir Luka Celestine**, one of the order’s greatest warriors. Luka’s death—at the hands of Filo’s own grandson, **Biliran**—marked the first true fracture in Filo’s reign.

---

### 🔥 **A Kingdom on the Edge**

Filo remains king, but his heart bears the weight of a divided family and a fragile peace.

- His heir, **Lance**, is dead.
    
- His chosen successor, **Lancia**, remains hidden at Strixhaven.
    
- His second son, **Belial**, smolders with ambition.
    
- And his grandson, **Biliran**, has already **spilled the blood of a knight**.
    

Filo continues to lead—graceful, dignified, and always just a few steps ahead. But those who watch closely can see the flicker behind his eyes: the question of whether the Hollow Throne can ever truly be filled without cracking.

---

### 🕊️ **Legacy in Motion**

Filo is not immortal, but his influence feels inevitable. His legacy is not in poems or tombs, but in **every knight who pauses**, **every noble who listens**, and **every citizen who believes that beauty and strength need not be opposites**.

And when the court bickers, or the army hesitates, or an assassin lowers their blade at the last moment, they all ask the same question:

> **“What would the Amereoux do?”**

And somehow, even in stillness,  
**Filo still dances.**

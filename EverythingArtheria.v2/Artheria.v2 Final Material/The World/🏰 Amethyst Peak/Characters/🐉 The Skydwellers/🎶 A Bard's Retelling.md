##  “The Victis Blood Burns Twice”**

_As sung in the Whispering Mug, a tavern near the border of Amethyst Peak and Aralon:_

> _“So gather ye close, by hearth and flame,_  
> _For I'll sing ye the tale of the Victorie name._  
> _From the peak of the world where the dragons once bowed,_  
> _Rose a line of kings—both broken and proud.”_

> __"First came Anarch, the god with no heart,_  
> Who ruled with a fire that tore peace apart.  
> His curse took root in the mountain stone,_  
> Where power would rot if it stood alone."**

> __"Then came Lazay, the feeder of hands,_  
> Who ruled by appeasing, ignoring commands.  
> With dragons he dozed—till the people did bite,_  
> And Victorie rose to set wrongs to right."**

> __"Oh Tristan the Strong, with blade like a hymn,_  
> Cut through the tyrant and wore the crown grim.  
> Undefeated in war, yet love laid him low,_  
> When grief struck swift with a silent blow."**

> _“His wife, his son, and kin all slain,_  
> Save two small twins who still remained.  
> A boy born bright, with a haloed gaze,*  
> And a girl whose flame was denied all praise.”*

> __“Now Bon walks tall, with sponsored cheers,_  
> But he fights not foes—he fights his fears.  
> For his sister turned shadow, her blade now free,_  
> And the boy wears chains none else can see.”**

> __“The crown wants a puppet, a godkin to wield,_  
> But beware the blood of the Victis Shield.  
> For glory is heavy, and dragons still sleep—_*  
> **And the sky remembers the promises we keep.”**

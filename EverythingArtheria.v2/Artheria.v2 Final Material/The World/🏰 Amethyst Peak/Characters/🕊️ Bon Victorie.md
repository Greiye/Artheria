### 🏔️ **The Golden Bastion of Amethyst Peak**

Bon Victorie is the grandson of the legendary **King Tristen Victorie**, known throughout the realm as _“The Victis.”_  
As a child, Bon survived a royal tragedy that claimed the lives of his family—one of the darkest nights in the history of **Amethyst Peak**. Alongside his twin sister, **Cela**, he was raised in the remnants of glory by the very king who had once commanded armies and tamed dragons.

When King Tristen eventually passed, Bon and Cela were left in the care of the kingdom’s stewards. The people looked to Bon as a symbol of hope—**the last heir of the Sky Dwellers**, touched by celestial blood, _a true Godkin_.

Now in his prime, Bon is the **top soldier of Amethyst Peak**, known across the continent for his one-on-one combat prowess, theatrical entrances, and divine presence in battle. His most iconic move is the **“Roof Knock”**—a timed explosion followed by a winged superhero landing that shakes the colosseum and clears the smoke with holy flair.

### 🟥 **Alias:** _The Crimson Cavalier_

Bon’s strength is more than muscle—it is **myth made flesh**. When he enters the battlefield, the temperature spikes, the air bends, and a heatwave rolls off of him like a desert storm. As a **Storm Herald Barbarian**, he channels the power of Amethyst Peak’s sun-scorched cliffs and dragon-forged sands. His rage is radiant, hot, and unrelenting.

Adorned in his sponsor-patched suit—an iconic crimson-and-gold ensemble—he has earned the name **The Crimson Cavalier**, a symbol of pride, performance, and power. He's as much a figure of inspiration as he is of strength.

The people chant his name. The court sponsors his armor. Cities far and wide brand him a hero.  
But behind the cheers and firelight, Bon carries the weight of legacy—and a bond with his twin sister that time and tragedy have only complicated.

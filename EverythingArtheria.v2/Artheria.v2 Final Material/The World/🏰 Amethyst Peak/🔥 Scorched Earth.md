After the death of his firstborn, **Lance**, King **Teofilo Blackthorn (Filo)** is grief-stricken and politically pressured. His granddaughter **Lancia**, the true heir, remains hidden at **Strixhaven Academy**. To bring her home, Filo sends his grandson **Biliran**, a magma para-genasi, alongside one of his top knights, **Sir Luka Celestine**, and Luka’s squire, **Arthur Aiden**, to locate the ancient portal buried in **Pele’s Playground**.

However, **Belial**, Filo’s second son and Biliran’s father, delivers a secret, treacherous order:

> **Kill the knight. Kill your cousin. Take the throne.**

---

### ⚔️ **The Journey**

- The party travels through **sulfur caverns**—a deadly maze only Luka can navigate using his **dragonglass sword**, which glows in the presence of magic.
    
- Along the way, Biliran shows signs of arrogance and tension, especially toward Arthur and Luka.
    
- Magical sulfur bats attack. Biliran fights fiercely, but his disregard for teamwork becomes clear.
    
- They emerge in **Pele’s Playground**, a lava-scarred landscape hiding the glowing portal to Strixhaven.
    

---

### 💀 **The Betrayal**

- Tensions flare. Luka begins to suspect Biliran’s true intentions.
    
- Biliran accuses Luka of insulting his lineage and questions his loyalty to the throne.
    
- Luka draws his blade, warning Biliran: _“You’re your father’s son.”_
    
- In a brutal duel, **Biliran defeats Luka**, destroying his obsidian hand and ultimately **casting him into lava**.
    
- Luka’s final words: _“I wish you were on our side.”_
    

---

### 🩸 **The Blade Changes**

After Luka’s death, **his dragonglass sword changes from green to red**, revealing a magical trail visible only to Biliran—a choice between returning to Castle Blackthorn… or entering the portal to Strixhaven.

Biliran enters the portal, glimpsing **Lancia** on the other side.

---

### 🕊️ **The Final Witness**

Squire Arthur is presumed dead—but his **falcon familiar, Millenia**, survives and flies back to report the betrayal.

The session ends with a conversation between **Belial** and a shadowy advisor.  
Belial’s final words:

> **“Scorched Earth.”**

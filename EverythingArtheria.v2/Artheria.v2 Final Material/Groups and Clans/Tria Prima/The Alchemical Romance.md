
Also know as the Tria Prima. It is the triangular trade system between 3 settlements:
## 🌋 **[[Amethyst Peak Old Lore]]** — _Sulfur (native), imports salt & mercury_

### Native Use – **Sulfur**

- **Military**: Sulfur is refined into explosives, flame-based weaponry, and magical fire powder for enchanted artillery and weapons used by the elite Dragoon Guard.
    
- **Religious/Magical**: Used in purification rituals, especially for “burning away weakness”—a very _Lachesism_-driven ideology.
    
- **Alchemy**: Symbolizes the **soul**; sulfur is used in rites of rebirth and strength enhancement for elite warriors and champions.
    

### Imported Use:

- **Salt (from Aralon)**: Used in preservation of dragon meat, ceremonial salts for rituals, and for magically warding areas against corruption or decay.
    
- **Mercury (from Ranlean)**: Mixed with dragon blood to create liquid enchantments or to bind magical seals in relics and vaults.
    

---

## 🌾 **[[Aralon]]** — _Salt (native), imports sulfur & mercury_

### Native Use – **Salt**

- **Agriculture**: Advanced preservation techniques for produce and meats, key to its strong export economy.
    
- **Spiritual**: Salt circles are used in blessings and prayers for peace and constancy; salt is symbolic of purity and balance.
    
- **Alchemy**: Represents the **body**; Aralonian alchemists use salt to ground spells, especially healing magic and anti-aging tonics.
    

### Imported Use:

- **Sulfur (from Amethyst Peak)**: Carefully regulated; used in small amounts in protective charms and, controversially, to infuse martial arts or tools with "fiery" spirit.
    
- **Mercury (from Ranlean)**: Stored and distilled in water-warded flasks; used in rare healing salves and divinatory pools—only allowed by Aralon's most trusted sages.
    

---

## 🧪 **[[Ranlean Old]]** — _Mercury (native), imports sulfur & salt_

### Native Use – **Mercury**

- **Technology**: Used in liquid-circuit enchantment systems, lenses, and magical conductors. Ranlean’s mercury is vital to Ranlean Tech infrastructure.
    
- **Alchemy/Science**: Represents the **spirit**; mercury is used in thought-enhancing elixirs, mind-mapping tools, and dreamscaping tech.
    
- **Medicine**: Instrumental in potion stabilization and crafting vaccines or mind-soothing tonics.
    

### Imported Use:

- **Sulfur (from Amethyst Peak)**: Used to forge fire-resistant alloys and power energy cores for magical constructs.
    
- **Salt (from Aralon)**: Necessary in insulation of volatile tech and stabilizing enchanted tools. Also used in culinary exports and for mental clarity teas consumed by scholars.

## Bonus: **Shared Philosophies & Conflict Potential**

- **Alchemy Guilds** may exist across all three regions, each teaching different philosophies based on which of the Tria Prima they prioritize.
    
- **Cultural tensions** could rise if, say, Ranlean experiments with sulfur in a way that’s viewed as “irresponsible” by Amethyst Peak—or if Aralon refuses to sell salt to fuel Ranlean's thought-modification tech.

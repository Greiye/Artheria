**Founders of “[[EverythingArtheria.v2/Artheria.v2 Final Material/Groups and Clans/⚖️ The Darkwaters/Characters/The Jury|The Jury]]” mafia syndicate.**  
Born seconds apart but opposites in every way.

- **Evie Darkwater** – The _cold strategist_.  
	- Brilliant, meticulous, and always three moves ahead. She orchestrated the Jury’s infiltration into Ranlean’s legal system, forging shadow deals with precision.  
	- Known to quote obscure legal texts mid-threat.
- Signature Traits
	- **Frosted Horns:** Her horns are ridged with frost and always cold to the touch. A manifestation of her calculating nature.
	- **Eyes Like Legal Ink:** Black sclera with shifting white script across them, constantly drafting invisible legalese.
	- **Aura of Silence:** Her presence dulls ambient noise—people find themselves whispering without meaning to.
	
- **Dante Darkwater** – The _charming manipulator_.  
	- A silver-tongued dealmaker who could sell sin to a saint. Handled the public face of the Jury, schmoozing nobles, bribing officials, and making every lie sound like gospel. 
	- Rumored to have lovers in every district, including a few in high court.
- Signature Traits
	- **Eyes Always Glowing Red:** A side effect of his constant lying.
		- Dante’s eyes could glow red when he lies… and his eyes are_always_ red.
		- “They say if Dante’s eyes ever stop glowing, the world will finally hear a truth too dangerous to live with.”
	- **Voice with a Persuasive Echo:** His words carry a faint, layered echo—like someone agreeing with him from the shadows.
	- **Illusory Smile:** His grin never fades… even when he’s not smiling.**

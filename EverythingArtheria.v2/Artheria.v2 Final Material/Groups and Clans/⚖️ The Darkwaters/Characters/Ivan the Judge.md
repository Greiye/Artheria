The eldest and most “righteous.” Publicly a paragon of justice, but secretly ensured the court always ruled in favor of the family.

- Father of **Ripley Darkwater**.
- Grandfather of **Dickie Darkwater**, who grows up in ignorance of the family's shadowy legacy.
- Signature Traits
	- **Gavel-Horned:** His horns curve downward like a judge’s gavel, and when he slams his fist, the sound resonates like a courtroom bang.
	- **Voice of Finality:** When he delivers a sentence, his words can silence a room magically for a moment.
    - **Verdict Eyes:** When passing judgment, his eyes flash white with a vertical slit, like a parchment being torn open.

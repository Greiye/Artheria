Wood Elf Druid
The Lumbricimancer

**Core Concept** A towering wood elf druid, survivor of a massacred tribe, who now walks the wilds as a silent, vengeful force. He has bonded with decay—summoning creatures of writhing worms and wielding a cursed bow that feeds on flesh. His wrath is slow, methodical, and inevitable.

**Backstory**
“The Feast of Silence” The wind was wrong that morning. Barathrum had tracked an elk across the ridge, just beyond the song of the river. It was gone now—frightened, maybe. He would not eat that night. But he would never starve again. When he returned to the grove, the stench struck him first. Ash. Blood. Rot. His tribe was gone—slain, butchered. The fires still smoked, but the corpses were already writhing. Worms and beetles moved as one, as if summoned. As if waiting. Barathrum dropped to his knees. He did not scream. He did not weep. He watched. He listened. He heard the whispers from beneath the soil. And he answered. He buried his name that day and took another: Barathrum, an old elvish word for “the deep, devouring earth.” Now he hunts—not beasts, but those who brought ruin. His bow spreads decay. His beasts crawl from the underworld. His justice is slow, patient, and inevitable.

Druid Subclass: Circle of Decay (Custom Homebrew) We’ll make Barathrum’s worm-based powers feel real in-game with this homebrew druid circle. Level 2 – Rotcaller's Bond Your Wild Shape becomes twisted. When you summon beasts using Conjure Animals, they take on grotesque worm-ridden forms. You learn the Infestation cantrip (if you don’t already have it), and it doesn’t count against your total cantrips known. Level 6 – Feast of the Dead When a creature you summoned reduces an enemy to 0 HP, you can use your reaction to heal for 1d8 HP and regain a druid spell slot (up to level 2). Additionally, when you cast a spell that deals poison or necrotic damage, worms briefly erupt from the target’s wounds, reducing their movement by 10 feet until the end of your next turn. Level 10 – Swarm of Vengeance Once per long rest, you may summon a Swarm of Worms: a writhing mass that acts as a Huge beast under your command (custom stat block). It lasts for 1 minute and spreads difficult terrain wherever it moves. Level 14 – Avatar of Decay You are immune to poison and disease. As an action once per long rest, you can transform into a decaying version of yourself. For 1 minute, your melee attacks deal an additional 2d6 necrotic damage, and enemies within 10 feet have disadvantage on Constitution saves.

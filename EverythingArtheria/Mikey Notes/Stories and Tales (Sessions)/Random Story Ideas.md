
**Tall Wooden Gates**
Hundreds of times I pass these wooden gates.
As I get older, the taller and stronger they become. Gaps in the wood get filled with snow and mud. rain and hail. Every year more secure. Deeper in the ground.
As my hair gets white and sparse, the world outside gets more dangerous, and the walls seem to grow taller. Addressing the every growing threat.
A realization.
It was always dangerous. If it's not the hungry bear or the talons of crows, It's the poison in the water. Worse yet, a couple of humans. with glint in their eyes and steel on their belt.
Or maybe it's me getting weaker, and thus the danger getting bigger than me. The threat's not getting bigger. I'm getting smaller.

I wonder how many more times I'll pass through these gates. And which side I'll be on in my final moments.

## Professions/Classes/Occupations

No such thing as 'classes'. Simply your skills.

In Artheria, there are many profession of adventurer. They cannot be sequestered into a single description. 

Most Popular occupation are **Mercenaries**.




## The General Backgrounds
How most mercenaries come to be.

Certainly! Here’s the breakdown in a markdown table format:

| Background     | Industry      | Mercenary Evolution                                                                                                 |
| -------------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Envoy**      | Political     | Once a skilled negotiator, you turned to mercenary work when diplomacy failed, blending intrigue with martial prowess. |
| **Executioner**| Gig/War       | Trained in precise killing, you took your lethal talents to the battlefield, offering swift death to the highest bidder.|
| **Falconer**   | Gig           | A master of the skies, you became a mercenary scout, using your trained birds and keen eye to track enemies.         |
| **Farmer**     | Common        | Hardship turned you from tilling fields to wielding weapons, your strength and resilience now valued in the mercenary ranks. |
| **Firefighter**| Common        | Once saving lives from flames, you now charge into battle, fighting fires of war with the same bravery and endurance. |
| **Guild Member**| Guild        | Trained in craft and trade, you sought adventure, leveraging your connections and skills to become a mercenary leader. |
| **Janitor**    | Common        | Cleaning up after others turned into a different kind of job—clearing battlefields, with your adaptability serving you well. |
| **Lone Survivor**| Victim      | After losing everything, survival became your only skill. As a mercenary, you fight with a cold, calculated instinct. |
| **Loyal Priest**| Religion     | When faith turned to conflict, you traded sermons for steel, protecting your flock as a mercenary defender of the faithful. |
| **Mage Hunter**| Gig/War       | Trained to hunt rogue magic users, your expertise in countering sorcery made you a mercenary specializing in magical threats. |
| **Metal Worker**| Common       | Forging weapons for others wasn’t enough. Now, as a mercenary, you wield the tools you once made, bringing your craftsmanship to the battlefield. |
| **Monster Hunter**| Gig/War    | After years of tracking beasts, you turned to hunting men. A mercenary life offers you constant work in war-torn lands. |
| **Sleuth**     | Gig           | Investigating crimes sharpened your mind. As a mercenary, you unravel mysteries on the battlefield, finding weaknesses in your foes. |
| **Squire**     | War           | Once a servant to knights, you learned the ways of combat firsthand. Now you fight for yourself as a mercenary, free of noble ties. |
| **Unknown**    | ?             | With no past and no name, you joined the mercenary ranks, letting your skills speak for themselves, your history buried. |
| **Wronged Hero**| Victim       | Betrayed and seeking justice, you became a mercenary to right the wrongs of your past, fighting for vengeance or redemption. |

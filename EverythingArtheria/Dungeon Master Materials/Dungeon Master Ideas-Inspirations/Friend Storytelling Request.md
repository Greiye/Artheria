**Core Game Design Principles to Implement:**
To create a compelling experience, the game should adhere to the following principles:

1.  **Central Narrative Drive:** Establish a clear primary goal or **endeavor for the main character(s)** that propels the story forward.
2.  **Meaningful Challenges:** Introduce **conflicts and obstacles** that directly test the characters' commitment to their main endeavor.
3.  **Engaging Gameplay Loop:** Test the characters through a variety of fun **game mechanics**, including combat, social challenges, traps, and problem-solving scenarios.
4.  **Organic Worldbuilding:** Reveal the game's **lore and backstory naturally** through gameplay, environmental clues, and character interactions, avoiding exposition dumps ("show, don't tell").
5.  **Defined Conclusion:** Ensure the main storyline reaches a **clear resolution**, acknowledging whether the characters ultimately succeed or fail in their quest.

## Consistent Art Style Prompt – AI Assistance for DMs

When generating AI art for campaigns, maintain consistency across styles using these prompt guidelines:

|Scenario|Prompt Keywords and Phrases|
|---|---|
|**Realistic but Manga Style**|"Realistic but Manga, consistent with previous images, detailed, intricate."|
|**Fantasy Style**|"Fantasy, uniform style, same art style as before, elaborate, bold colors."|
|**Cartoon Style**|"Cartoon, maintain the same style, similar to previous works, simple, clean lines."|
|**Modern Style**|"Modern, consistent with previous images, minimalistic, clean lines, soft colors."|
|**Gothic Style**|"Gothic, uniform style, detailed, intricate, bold colors."|
|**Steampunk Style**|"Steampunk, maintain the same style, elaborate, same art style as before."|
|**Cyberpunk Style**|"Cyberpunk, consistent with previous images, detailed, bold colors, similar style."|

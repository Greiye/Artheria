"DnD Risk"
A Fun way to establish the world's country boundaries.

*   **Setup:**
    *   Suggests unit counts based on player numbers (35 units for 3 players, 30 for 4 players).
    *   Players take turns placing initial troops.
*   **Gameplay Mechanics:**
    *   Cards represent special landforms.
    *   Reinforcements are calculated based on territory count (Territories / 3).
    *   Turns consist of Pass/Move/Attack phases.
    *   Combat uses 3 dice, comparing the highest die roll from attacker and defender.
    *   Cards can grant additional reinforcements (1d4).
*   **Faction Unit Ideas:** potential unit types for different fantasy races:
    *   **Mermaid Units:** Trident Throwers, Siren Squadron (coral bows), Manta Ray Scouts (flying, limited range), Amphibious Assault Team, Mermaid Marines.
    *   **Dragon Units:** Talon Troopers, Jagged Edge Knights, Nightstalkers, Thunder Scouts, Brood Nurses.
    *   **Tiefling Army:** Bloodfiends, Shadow Spirits, Soul Eaters, Chaos Mages, Summoners.
    *   **Orcs:** Berzerk Battalion, Warboar Cavalry, Wardrum Shamans, Tusk Artillery, Stealtech Sappers.
